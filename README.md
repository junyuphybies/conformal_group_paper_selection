# Caltech Conformal Group Paper Selection

A selection of interesting hep-th papers on conformal field theory and related topics for the Caltech conformal group. The selections reflect Junyu Liu's personal taste and interests; they are not an objective ranking or a judgment of the quality of selected or unselected papers.

Maintained by Junyu Liu.

Paper lists use the filename pattern `YYYY_Mon_N.txt`, where `Mon` is the three-letter month abbreviation determined by the papers' posting dates, and `N` is the selection wave within that month, starting at 1. For example, `2026_Nov_1.txt` is the first November 2026 wave, and `2026_Nov_2.txt` is the second. The wave number is not a day of the month, and the month is not determined by when the papers were collected.

Each text file contains the selected paper links, followed by the posted date range and the specific posting days covered.
